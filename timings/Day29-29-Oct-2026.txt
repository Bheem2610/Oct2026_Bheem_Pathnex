SRE stands for Site Reliability Engineering. It is a way of using software engineering, automation, and monitoring to keep applications and IT services reliable, available,

Important SRE concepts

1. SLI — Service Level Indicator

A measurement of how well a service is performing.

2. SLO — Service Level Objective

The reliability target the team wants to achieve.

3. SLA — Service Level Agreement

An agreement with customers that defines the expected service level and what happens if the commitment is not met.

4. Error Budget

The amount of unreliability allowed by an SLO.

5. Toil

Repetitive, manual operational work that can potentially be automated.

5. SRE tools

=>Monitoring: Prometheus and Grafana

Collect metrics and visualize system health, resource utilization, and service performance.

=>Logging: ELK Stack

Search and analyze logs to investigate failures and identify root causes.

=>Automation: Ansible, Bash, Python

Automate configuration management, repetitive checks, and routine operations.

=>Containers and orchestration: Kubernetes

Manage containerized applications, health checks, scaling, and recovery.

=>CI/CD: Jenkins and Git

Automate software build, testing, and deployment workflows.
